Model scope & assumptions

Mechanism: Each attempted kit enters one of two lanes, passes the box-forming/entry zone, and reaches the robotic pick-and-place station. Skew and sticking are probabilistic fault events. The Option 1 jig, gate and guides reduce the modelled probability of these two fault types; gripper-drop risk is unchanged.

Calibration: Baseline event probabilities use 15, 7 and 1 events divided by 1,049 reference attempts. For this illustrative model, skew damages one kit and creates a 20 s stoppage, sticking creates a 20 s stoppage, and a gripper drop creates a 15 s stoppage. These durations are assumptions, not measured event-level downtime.

Important: Observed Run B loss of roughly 455 seconds per 1,000 kits from the report is a contextual upper-limit estimate, not a verified prediction of achievable savings. Run A and Run B represent different operating conditions and should not be treated as a controlled before/after experiment.

Verification pathway

Stage 1 → Stage 2

Baseline logging, physical geometry inspection, feasibility review of jig/entry gate/guide clearances, and operator access assessment.

Stage 3 → Stage 4

Controlled Partial Pack trials, then Full Two-Pack validation. Compare fault rates, quality, cycle time and intervention frequency under matched operating conditions before making performance claims.
