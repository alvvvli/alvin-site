# Cirque du Soleil — KÀ: Automation and Precision in Large-Format Live Systems

<div class="hero">

  <p class="hero__caption">TAIT Navigator · FTSI Automation · Meyer Sound + Solotech Integration</p>
</div>

**KÀ**, at MGM Grand in Las Vegas, remains one of the most technically demanding productions in Cirque du Soleil’s portfolio.  
The entire show is powered by a **large-format motion-control environment** that operates at tolerances closer to industrial automation than performing arts.

At the center is the **TAIT Navigator** platform, orchestrating multi-axis stage lifts, rotational decks, tracked gantries, and performer automation.  
These systems run in real time, with positional feedback routed into **FTSI’s safety and cue management backbone** to maintain synchronized, fail-safe operation for hundreds of cues per performance.

Complementing the mechanical architecture is a **Meyer Sound spatial audio field**, integrated and tuned through **Solotech’s networked control framework**.  
Sound objects track performer motion, vertical elevation, and rotational orientation — allowing acoustics to move with the narrative and mechanical choreography.

The result is a **cyber-physical performance system**: a continuously running, human-in-the-loop automation environment that blends theatrical design with industrial system reliability.
