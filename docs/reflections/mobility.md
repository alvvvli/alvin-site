# Global Mobility Network

Over the years, I've spent time living, studying, working, and travelling across different parts of the world. Taiwan, California, the Netherlands, Denmark, and Singapore have been recurring bases, with many shorter journeys in between.

This map brings together those places and the routes connecting them, from intercontinental flights to regional rail journeys and local excursions.

## Along the Way

Much of my travel has taken shape around university exchanges, conferences, industrial visits, and opportunities to explore independently.

Some journeys have involved familiar routes between places I've lived. Others have been short trips by train, bus, or air, often with no particular agenda beyond seeing somewhere new.

I've kept this as a small visual record of where I've been and how those places connect.
