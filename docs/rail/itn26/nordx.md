The FLIRT Nordic Express offered an interesting look at long-distance passenger comfort within a multiple-unit platform. Inside, I explored the seating arrangements, generous legroom, folding tables, and electronically adjustable seats.

I also had the opportunity to examine the driver's cab, including its control desk, operating displays, and integrated communication equipment. The contrast between the passenger environment and the operational workspace was particularly interesting: two very different interfaces designed around the same railway vehicle.
