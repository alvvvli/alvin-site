The Stadler RS ZERO provided a different perspective on railway modernisation, particularly for regional routes where conventional electrification may be difficult to justify.

I explored the vehicle from underneath the chassis through to the driver's cab, examining its underfloor equipment, control interfaces, driving handle, and passenger accommodation.

What interested me most was the operational side of the design: how a relatively compact regional train integrates propulsion technology, driver ergonomics, and accessible passenger spaces while accommodating the infrastructure constraints of secondary railway lines.
