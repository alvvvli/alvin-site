CRRC's exhibits stood out for their unconventional exterior geometry, large windows, and contemporary passenger interiors.

Walking through the vehicles, I examined the information displays, door arrangements, seating configurations, and expansive window designs. The exhibition also provided an opportunity to observe how CRRC presents its vision of future passenger rail through both engineering and industrial design.

The experience offered an interesting contrast with the European rolling stock elsewhere at InnoTrans, particularly in the relationship between passenger-facing technology, interior architecture, and vehicle styling.
