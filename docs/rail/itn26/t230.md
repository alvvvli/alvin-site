The Korean EMU-320 was especially interesting as an example of high-speed rail development outside the established European manufacturers.

I explored the passenger saloon, including its overhead information displays, seatback amenities, charging provisions, and interior arrangement. Seeing the train up close also offered a better appreciation of its aerodynamic nose design and overall vehicle proportions.

Beyond the headline speed capabilities, I was interested in the details that make high-speed rail practical for everyday passengers: onboard information, seating comfort, and the integration of amenities into a space-efficient interior.
