Having experienced the London Underground as a passenger, it was particularly interesting to step inside Siemens' new Piccadilly line rolling stock before its introduction into regular service.

The walk-through offered a closer look at the train's distinctive exterior profile, passenger circulation space, seating layout, and information displays. Its design illustrates the challenges of introducing modern accessibility, capacity, and passenger amenities within the physical constraints of an established underground railway network.
