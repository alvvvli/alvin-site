# MRO at Singapore Airshow 2026: keeping a growing fleet in the air
Twenty years after its 2008 debut, this 10th edition made one thing obvious: Asia-Pacific's MRO sector has grown from a support act into one of the show's core pillars, right alongside advanced air mobility and space.

## What the maintenance world announced
- **ST Engineering consolidates airframe and nacelle work** — a new integrated MRO service centre at its Singapore facilities lets operators combine airframe and nacelle maintenance work-scopes in a single location, aimed squarely at cutting operational complexity and turnaround time.
- **Boeing and ANA go deeper on predictive maintenance** — the two expanded their existing partnership on predictive aircraft maintenance, part of the broader industry shift from scheduled to data-driven, condition-based servicing.
- **MRO sits at the centre of the show's identity now** — organiser Experia Events explicitly listed MRO and Aftermarket Services as one of Singapore Airshow 2026's core visitor-profile pillars, alongside civil and defence aviation, space, and advanced air mobility — a formal recognition of how central the sector has become to the show.
- **The regional growth case writes itself** — Asia-Pacific accounted for over half of global air traffic growth in 2025, and Singapore's aviation sector has contributed more than S$750 million to the economy since the 2024 show, with hundreds more jobs expected over the next five years, largely on the back of MRO and supply-chain expansion.
- **A dedicated follow-up event is already on the calendar** — MRO Asia-Pacific 2026 returns to Singapore on 22–24 September, expecting more than 7,500 attendees and 350-plus exhibitors, giving the sector a second, MRO-only stage later this year.
