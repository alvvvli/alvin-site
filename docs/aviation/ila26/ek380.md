# Emirates A380: the crowd magnet on an increasingly military tarmac
Every ILA needs one aircraft that stops non-aviation people in their tracks, and in 2026 that job fell to Emirates' A380 — parked on a static park that was otherwise leaning harder into defence hardware than in past editions.

## Notes from the static park
- **Still the biggest thing on the tarmac** — the show's own billing called it out plainly: the Emirates A380 as the world's largest passenger aircraft, parked alongside the Airbus Beluga transport and a Lufthansa A320neo in a special "100 Years of Lufthansa" livery.
- **A genuine public draw** — German coverage of the opening days led with the size of the crowds around the A380, even as the broader show narrative shifted toward defence and security themes this year.
- **Emirates' scale advantage** — the airline operates the largest A380 fleet in the world, and its ILA presence functions as a long-haul counterpoint to a static park otherwise dominated by fighters, transports and drones.
- **Part of the furniture** — Emirates has been a recurring ILA exhibitor; the airline's A380 appearance in past editions has come configured across all four of its cabin classes for public viewing, giving visitors a rare close-up of first-class suites and the onboard shower spa without needing a boarding pass.
